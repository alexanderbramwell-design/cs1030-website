About the London System

The London system is a classical chess opening developed with the idea of controlling the e4 square, allowing kingside or queenside castling, developing the dark squared bishop outside the pawn chain, and preparing for an e4 pawn break.

It is a solid line that can teach core principles that are found in many openings in chess, mainly a strong pawn structure, and overprotection of central squares.

Typical Moves

  1. 1. d4 d5
  2. 2. Bf4 Nf6
  3. 3. e3 e6
